caucus-pulse — build specification

For Astra · a pixel-faithful build of the Dashboard and Incident desk · September 8, 2026 · reference files: DashboardV3.dc.html, Incidents.dc.html

This document describes the two screens exactly as designed so they can be rebuilt as a static site (GitHub Pages) fed by rollups.json from the caucus-pulse pipeline. Everything below — colors, type, spacing, component shapes, metric definitions and behaviors — is meant to be copied, not interpreted. Where the reference HTML and this document disagree, the HTML wins.

Feed (right column, height follows the Topics card)

Header: “Feed”, ink dot + “16 posts”, Newest / Top segmented control. One dropdown: topics with counts. Body scrolls internally (scrollbar-gutter: stable). Each post: 30px disc with white 11px/700 initials — green #00823a for CPC members, navy #194292 for New Dem, gray #8e8e93 for everyone else (CPC wins a tie); the identity caucuses show only on the dot tags beside the handle; handle (13/600) · caucus dot + label (10px) · time right; text 13px/1.4; up to two topic tags plus “+n”; engagement right (“↻” prefix for reposts, “❝” for quotes). Rows separated by 1px #f0f0f2.

Phrases

Caption beside the title, 11px gray: “shared 2–4-word lines · gauged by adoption, unity across caucuses, and discipline of wording”. Columns: Phrase · Pillar · Origin · Adoption · Unity · Discipline. Pillar is the core message the line serves — Affordability, Healthcare or Corruption, the same three as the Core-messages card — as a #f2f2f4 tag, with the fine topic in 11px gray beside it on the same line (omitted when it merely repeats the pillar name); a line serving none of the three carries a dashed “Off-pillar” tag (gray text, 1px dashed #d2d2d7). Origin is first user · date, plus “· leadership” when the first user holds an elected leadership post. Adoption is the member count (600), a 64×18 curve of cumulative adopters over seven days scaled from zero (plateaus read flat, new lines climb from the baseline), and the seven-day gain (“+51”: ink 500 at ≥ 5, #8e8e93 below). Unity is five 7px dots in caucus order (CPC, New Dem, CBC, CHC, CAPAC), filled in the caucus color when the line reaches at least three of that caucus’s members and at least 10% of its active members, #e8e8ed otherwise, followed by “3/5”; the tooltip carries the counts. Discipline is the share of uses in the exact wording (“81%”), ink 600 when under 75% — the line is drifting — and gray otherwise; the tooltip lists the variants. Engagement is deliberately absent: it follows the poster, not the wording.

Emerging

Per cluster: name + “since 10:05 AM today”; one line with posts · members · engagement (14px/700 numbers, 12px gray labels) and a 72×18 growth curve; a 5px caucus bar; the sample quote in gray; then “58% say ‘Fed independence’”, the suggested taxonomy tag, and Compose / Add ›.

Breaking in district

Card title with “3 active”; right link “Open incident desk ›”. 3-up grid (min 290px) of #f7f7f9 cards: red kind tag, “12:48 PM → 2:10 PM”; place (14/600) and handle · name; quote in #3a3a3c; footer “3 updates · 9.7K engagement” and “Thread ›”.

Compose (modal)

Backdrop rgba(29,29,31,.38); dialog white, radius 16, max 960px, shadow 0 24px 70px rgba(0,0,0,.28). Left column: Subject dropdown (topics, phrases, incidents, emerging clusters), Scope (caucus dropdown + Today/7 days), Include checkboxes (@handle, District, Caucus, Posts & engagement, Best quote, Time of last post, Numbered, Source line), Order (Engagement / Caucus / Name), Format (List / Brief / Handles only), and “Also run an X search”. Output is plain text: header line, one person per line, quotes trimmed to 110 characters, optional “—” source footer. No markdown, no emoji.

4. Metric definitions

Momentum (0–100, 50 = steady) = 40% volume lift + 20% acceleration + 20% member adoption + 10% caucus spread + 10% engagement lift. Each component maps to 0–1 with 0.5 neutral: volume = today vs 7-day average (±50% saturates); acceleration = slope of the last two days minus the slope of the first four, over 10% of the average; adoption = distinct members vs their 7-day baseline (±50% saturates); spread = (effective number of caucuses − 1) / 2 where effective = 1/Σ share²; engagement lift = engagement per post vs its baseline (±50%). Leader = highest score; drivers = the two largest components.
MetricDefinition
PostsAll captured posts in the window for the scope; reposts and replies counted, shown in the composition bar. A “reposts” tweak excludes them (×0.58).
Core messagesPosts classified into Affordability, Healthcare, or Corruption (Epstein files + GOP reconciliation law); share = of all classified posts.
EngagementLikes + reposts + replies + quotes from the 24-hour re-read; per post = total / posts; top-10 share = engagement on the ten highest members / total.
Topic caucus splitPosts by members tagged to that caucus; members with several tags count in each.
Phrase adoption / unity / disciplineA phrase family = a canonical 2–4-gram plus its variants (one token edit or the same head noun), clustered nightly. Adoption = distinct members who used the family in the last 7 days; the curve is cumulative distinct members by day; gain = day 7 minus day 1. Unity = caucuses where adopters are ≥ 3 members and ≥ 10% of the caucus’s active accounts (members with several tags count in each). Discipline = uses of the exact canonical wording ÷ all uses of the family. Pillar is assigned by the classifier from the same three core messages as the stat card; anything else is off-pillar. Leadership = elected House Democratic leadership (Leader, Whip, Caucus Chair and Vice Chair, DCCC and DPCC chairs).
Emerging clusterUnclassified posts grouped by embedding similarity nightly; coherence = share of posts containing the cluster's most common phrase.
IncidentClassifier-flagged district emergency posts; excluded from topic counts; status active → monitoring → resolved (no member posts for 36h).

5. Data contract

The pages read one file, rollups.json, rebuilt every poll. Shape (abridged; see the static D object in the reference file for the exact keys):

{
  built: "2026-09-07T18:37:00Z", nextPoll: "…", accounts: 251,
  topics: [{ key, name, c: [cpc, newdem, cbc, chc, capac], eng, m, mAvg, epAvg, trend: [7], d,
             subs: [[key, [cpc, newdem, cbc, chc, capac], eng, members, leadHandle]] }],
  phrases: [{ text, pillar, topic, first, when, leadership, spread, cm: [cpc, newdem, cbc, chc, capac], adopt: [7], exact, variants: [], total }],   // pillar null = off-pillar; adopt = cumulative members by day
  clusters: [{ label, posts, members, cm, eng, phrase, coherence, since, shape: [8], sample, suggest }],
  incidents: [{ id, kind, status, title, place, handle, member, since, last, updates, engN,
                timeline: [{ time, who, tag, official, unverified, text, meta, isNew }],
                confirmed: [{ text, src }], unverified: [{ text, src }], whatsNew: [], others: [] }],
  feed: [{ handle, member, caucus: [], text, topics: [], time, engN, kind }],
  members: { "@handle": [displayName, district, [caucuses]] }
}

Times are shown in US-Eastern; store UTC. Keys in snake_case render through a labeler (Title Case, with ACA / ICE / GOP / DC / USPS / FEMA kept uppercase).

6. Acceptance checklist

  • Filters (caucus, window) change every number, the H1 color, and hide the caucus columns when a single caucus is selected.
  • No caucus hue appears on links, buttons or status indicators; green belongs to CPC alone; red is shared by CBC and alerts, and alerts always pair it with an uppercase label.
  • All text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast; orange (CHC) fills carry ink text, every other caucus fill carries white; secondary text is #6e6e73, never lighter, for anything informational.
  • Feed height equals the Topics card height; the feed scrolls internally and its right edge is never clipped by the scrollbar.
  • Compose output copies as plain text and pastes into Signal with line breaks intact; “Copied ✓” confirms.
  • Both pages lay out cleanly from 920px to 1440px; nothing in the sticky header wraps.
  • Numbers are tabular; trend lines are smooth curves with an end dot; bars are 3–5px with 2–3px radii.
